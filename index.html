Paddy Disease Guide

Learn about common paddy leaf diseases, their symptoms, and preventive measures.

Healthy Leaf

A healthy paddy leaf is vibrant green, smooth, and free from spots or lesions. Crucial for photosynthesis and optimal grain yield.

✅ No Treatment Needed

Brown Spot

Fungal disease caused by Cochliobolus miyabeanus. Oval to circular brown spots with yellow halos. Causes significant yield loss.

🍄 Fungal Disease

Hispa

Caused by Dicladispa armigera. Adults scrape leaf surface; grubs mine inside leaves creating white blotchy streaks.

🐛 Insect Pest

Bacterial Leaf Blight

Caused by Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae. Water-soaked yellowish lesions along leaf margins. One of the most destructive diseases.

🦠 Bacterial Disease

Leaf Smut

Caused by Entyloma oryzae. Small angular black spots on leaf blades. Generally minor but can reduce photosynthesis efficiency.

🍄 Fungal Disease

Leaf Blast

Caused by Magnaporthe oryzae. Diamond-shaped lesions with gray centers and brown borders. Highly destructive, favored by cool temperatures.

🍄 Fungal Disease
